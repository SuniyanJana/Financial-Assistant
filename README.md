# 💸 Financial Assistant

### Your money. One place. Zero guesswork.

> **Financial Assistant** is a personal finance dashboard built to turn everyday financial activity into something you can actually understand.

Instead of jumping between expense notes, investment apps, spreadsheets and mental calculations, Financial Assistant brings the important pieces together in one dashboard.

Track where your money goes.  
Understand what remains.  
Watch your investments.  
Set goals.  
And keep an eye on your financial health.

---

## ✦ The Idea

Managing money isn't difficult because people can't calculate.

It's difficult because the information is scattered.

An expense happens here.  
An investment happens there.  
A savings goal exists somewhere else.  
And at the end of the month, the question is:

> **"Where did my money actually go?"**

Financial Assistant was built around that question.

The application connects **expenses, available funds, investments and goals** into one personal financial workspace.

# 🧭 Inside the Dashboard

Financial Assistant is organized around four main areas:

### 💳 Expenses

Record and understand your spending instead of simply storing numbers.

- Add and manage expenses
- Categorize spending
- View spending breakdowns
- Analyze monthly spending
- Visualize expenses through charts
- Track spending patterns

---

### 💰 Fund Tracker

Your financial control center.

The Fund Tracker brings together:

- Monthly income
- Current balance
- Monthly spending
- Previous-month spending
- Daily spending average
- Estimated days your balance can last
- Emergency-fund estimation

It also allows your income to be updated directly from the application.

---

### 📈 Investments

Keep your investments connected to your overall financial picture.

Track:

- Investment name / asset
- Amount invested
- Current value
- Expected return rate
- Active investments
- Withdrawn investments
- Investment history

The application also considers active investments when calculating available funds.

---
